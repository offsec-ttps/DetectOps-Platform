# Detection

**Sidebar section:** Detection

The Detection section is your detection inventory and validation surface. It maps to the dashboard's **Detection** menu.

## In this section

| Subsection | Route | What it does |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| [Detections](../engineering/detections.md) | `/detections` | Central inventory of your detection rules and use cases. |
| [Detection Packs](detection-packs.md) | `/detections/packs` | AI-generated bundles of Sigma rules + IR playbooks. |
| [Detection Validation](detection-validation.md) | `/detection-validation` | Check that detection logic fires against your SIEM. |

## How it connects

- Author/adopt rules in [Detections](../engineering/detections.md) (borrow from [Community Content](../content/community-content.md)).
- Generate bundles in [Detection Packs](detection-packs.md) from a rule or [threat intel](../content/threat-aggregator.md).
- Prove coverage in [Detection Validation](detection-validation.md) and via [Enterprise Use-Case Validation](../simulation/enterprise-use-cases.md).
