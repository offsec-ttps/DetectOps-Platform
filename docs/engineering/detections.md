# Detections

**Route:** `/detections` · **Sidebar:** Engineering → Detections

The central inventory of your detection rules and use cases — the "source of truth" for detections you own, separate from the read-only community catalog.

## What it does

- Browse and manage your detection rules and use cases (`/detections`, detail at `/detections/[id]`).
- Track custom detections (`/api/custom-detections`).
- Sync and verify Splunk detections (`/api/splunk-detections` + `/sync`, `/updates`, `/verify`).

## APIs

`/api/detections`, `/api/custom-detections`, `/api/splunk-detections` (+ `/sync`, `/updates`, `/verify`).
